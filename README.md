# The Great 8b Cooking Lesson 👩‍🍳

An interactive 3D kitchen (three.js) for the English unit **"Good food"**, made for iPads.

- **a) Sort the words**: put the food words from the box into the table (vegetables, meat, dairy products, other), then add your own words.
- **b) Cook a meal**: Partner A makes spaghetti with tomato sauce, Partner B makes chicken curry with rice, and there are 4 more recipes. Find the ingredients, follow the English instructions (chop, fry, stir, boil …) and serve.
- ⏩ Time speed-up (1×, 10×, 60×, 300×) for the cooking timers.
- 🗣️ Words and steps read aloud (British English), 🔊 synthesized kitchen sounds.
- With "Words" switched off, the ingredients show pictures only (harder challenge).
- At the end: a ready-made sentence for *"Tell your partner your ingredients"* and the steps with First / Then / Next / Finally.

No build step: static files, served by GitHub Pages. Tip: on the iPad, use *Share → Add to Home Screen* for full screen.
